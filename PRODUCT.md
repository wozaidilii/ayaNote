# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Japanese 1v1 teachers (primary: Ayano) preparing and reviewing lessons between classes — often on a laptop at a desk, under bright indoor light, switching quickly between calendar, student memory, and Meet transcripts. Students use a lite portal to book and see homework.

## Product Purpose

AyaNote is an AI-assisted lesson memory workspace: pull Meet/Drive transcripts into structured summaries, keep per-student vocab/grammar progress, draft next-class prep, and sync bookings from Google Calendar. Success = the teacher opens Today or a student profile and immediately sees what to teach next without hunting notes.

## Brand Personality

Calm · Precise · Workmanlike

Feels like a trusted teaching desk tool (Notion/Linear density), not a study brand or EdTech marketing site. Quiet confidence; Japanese + English bilingual labels without theatrical “learn Japanese!” energy.

## Anti-references

- EdTech marketing landings (hero illustrations, purple gradients, rounded-full pill forests)
- “Study desk” cream / terracotta / serif-display vibes
- Game/pixel UI, glow effects, glassmorphism dashboards
- Identical SaaS metric card grids and decorative side-stripes

## Design Principles

1. **Task first** — every screen should get the teacher into the next action (open lesson, sync calendar, seed memory) in one glance.
2. **Dense but scannable** — prefer compact lists and calendars over large empty marketing space.
3. **Familiar tool grammar** — side nav, panels, chips, and buttons should feel like Linear/Notion/Confluence neighbors, not invented affordances.
4. **Accent is signal** — blue only for primary actions, selection, and status; never decoration wallpaper.
5. **Bilingual clarity** — labels stay short; hierarchy over flourish.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA: ≥4.5:1 body contrast, visible focus rings, 44×44px touch targets on mobile controls, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected. Support ja/en locale switching without layout breakage.
